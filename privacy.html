Privacy Policy

How Teo’s Catering collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our website and contact us.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Our Commitment

Teo’s Catering (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with applicable Canadian privacy laws, including PIPEDA.

Information We Collect

  • Name and contact information submitted through forms
  • Email address and phone number
  • Website usage data through cookies and tracking technologies

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to catering inquiries
  • To improve our website and services
  • For marketing and advertising purposes (if consent is provided)

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and technologies such as Meta Pixel to understand website traffic and improve advertising performance. These tools may collect anonymous usage data. Tracking only occurs if you consent via our cookie banner.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Meta (Facebook) for analytics and advertising.

Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, deliver requested services, maintain required business records, and meet legal obligations. Information is securely deleted or anonymized when no longer required.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, or loss. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take privacy protection seriously.

Your Rights & Choices

You may withdraw consent at any time by clicking “Withdraw Cookie Consent” in the footer of our website. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal requirements.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
